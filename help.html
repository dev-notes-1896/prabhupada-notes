Help and feedback

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email prabhupadanotes@gmail.com, or open an issue on the project’s GitHub page. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal information there.

Common questions

The Notes button doesn’t appear

It shows on verse pages of the supported books: Bhagavad-gītā, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta, The Nectar of Instruction, Śrī Īśopaniṣad and Śrī Brahma-saṁhitā. Prefaces, introductions and other books aren’t supported yet. If you have just installed or updated the extension, reload the page.

It says “Not backed up”

Your notes are saved in this browser only. Click the Prabhupada Notes icon in Chrome’s toolbar and choose Sign in to back up. If it says Google Drive couldn’t be reached, check your internet connection and press Try again.

Seeing your notes on another computer

Install the extension there and sign in with the same Google account. Or choose Export as JSON in the toolbar popup, then Restore from file on the other computer. Restoring only adds notes; it never deletes notes or replaces newer versions.

Removing your notes

The privacy policy’s Your choices section explains how to export your notes, sign out, delete the copy in your Google Drive, and remove the extension’s access to your Google account.
