Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: your notes stay in your browser and, only if you choose to sign in, in a private folder of your own Google Drive. There is no Prabhupada Notes server. The developer never receives your notes or anything else about you. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Prabhupada Notes is a free browser extension for writing personal notes on the verses you read at vedabase.io. This policy explains what the extension stores, where, and what control you have. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust or the Bhaktivedanta Archives (VedaBase).

What the extension stores

DataWhy
The text of notes you writeIt is your note.
The passage you selected, which section it is in (for example, “Purport”), and a few words around itTo show the note beside the passage, and highlight it again when you come back.
Which verse the note belongs to, and when it was created or editedTo list and order your notes.
An ID and date for each deleted note (no text)So a deleted note stays deleted when your devices sync.
Settings, such as whether you are signed in, when notes were last backed up, and tips you have dismissedTo show the right status and not repeat tips.

The extension does not collect your browsing history, your name, your email address, or analytics, and it does not use cookies, advertising, or tracking.

Where your notes are kept

In your browser, always

Notes are saved in the extension’s own storage in Chrome, on your computer, for your Chrome profile. Websites cannot read it. Notes kept only here are lost if you uninstall the extension or clear Chrome’s data for it, so the extension warns you while notes are not backed up.

In your Google Drive, only if you sign in

If you choose “Sign in to back up”, a copy of your notes is saved in the app data folder of your own Google Drive. This is a hidden folder that only Prabhupada Notes can use. It does not appear among your files, and it counts toward your Drive storage (notes take very little space). Your notes then go directly between your browser and Google; they never pass through any server run by the developer.

Your Google account

Signing in asks Google for one permission: drive.appdata, which lets the extension see, create, and delete its own data in your Drive. With it, the extension cannot see, open, or change any of your other Drive files, and it has no access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or profile. It does not learn your email address.

Sign-in is handled by Chrome. The temporary access token Chrome provides is used only to talk to Google Drive and is never sent anywhere else. When you sign out, the extension asks Google to revoke its access.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Prabhupada Notes’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Web pages

The extension runs only on vedabase.io. To place highlights and save the passage you select, it reads the text of the verse page in your browser. It does not send page content or the addresses of pages you visit anywhere.

Network connections

The extension connects only to Google: to sign in, to read and write your notes in your Drive’s app data folder, and to revoke access when you sign out. It loads no remote code and contacts no other servers.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or to train machine-learning models. The developer has no access to your notes.

Your choices

  • Export your notes at any time from the extension’s toolbar popup (“Export as JSON”), and restore them from that file later.
  • Sign out from the toolbar popup. You can keep your notes in the browser, or remove them from it; your Drive copy is not deleted by signing out.
  • Delete your Drive copy: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Prabhupada Notes → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove access at any time from your Google Account: Your connections to third-party apps & services.
  • Delete everything in your browser by uninstalling the extension.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, scripts, analytics, or third-party fonts. GitHub may log visits (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change would affect how your notes are handled, it will be described here before an extension update puts it into effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email prabhupadanotes@gmail.com, or open an issue on the project’s GitHub page (issues are public, so please don’t include personal information there).
