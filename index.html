Prabhupada Notes

Write notes on the verses you study at vedabase.io. Select a passage, add your realization, and it’s waiting for you the next time you read that verse.

Add to Chrome Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store · Free

Notes on the passage

Select any words in the verse, translation or purport and choose Add note. You can also write a note on the whole verse.

Highlights that come back

Noted passages are gently highlighted. Hover to read your note; click to open all notes for the verse.

Private by design

Notes stay in your browser. Sign in to back them up to your own Google Drive and see them on your other computers.

How it works

  1. Open any verse on vedabase.io.
  2. Select a few words. An Add note button appears beside them.
  3. Write your note and press Save. The passage is highlighted from now on.
  4. Open the Notes button at the bottom right of the page to see, edit, or delete the verse’s notes. The toolbar popup lets you search all your notes.

Supported books

  • Bhagavad-gītā As It Is
  • Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam
  • Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta
  • The Nectar of Instruction
  • Śrī Īśopaniṣad
  • Śrī Brahma-saṁhitā

Combined verses (such as Bg. 1.16–18) are supported too.

Your notes are yours

There is no Prabhupada Notes server. Notes are saved in your browser, and, only if you sign in, in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive that this extension alone can use. It cannot see any of your other files. Nothing is sold, shared, or tracked.

Read the privacy policy

Without signing in, notes stay on this computer only, and the extension tells you so. You can always export your notes to a file and restore them later.
